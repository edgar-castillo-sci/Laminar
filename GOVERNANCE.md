# Governance

## Model

Laminar is a **founder-led** project. The founder is the sole maintainer with
authority over the direction, the public API, the acceptance of contributions,
and the use of the project name.

This model is intentionally simple for the specification phase and is
inspired by the BDFL model used by Linux, Python, and other long-lived
scientific software projects. It may evolve, but only by explicit decision
of the founder.

## Founder

**Edgar Castillo** — founder, lead maintainer, copyright
holder of the original codebase, and owner of the Laminar name.

## Scope

Laminar is an **independent software project**. It implements the JAM
notation, which was originally proposed by Arcudia et al. (2023), but the
project itself — its code, its architecture, its API, and its roadmap — is
governed solely by the founder.

- **JAM notation** is a scientific standard, cited in the literature.
- **Laminar** is a software suite. It is not the JAM paper, and it does not
  claim authorship over the notation.

## Decision process

1. **Proposals**: anyone may open an issue with a proposal.
2. **Decision**: the founder decides whether to accept, defer, or reject.
3. **Contributions**: code contributions are accepted under the terms of
   `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
4. **Appeals**: if a contributor disagrees with a decision, the founder
   is the final authority. This is by design: the project is founder-led.

## Roles

- **Founder / Maintainer**: Edgar Castillo. Final authority
  on all decisions.
- **Contributors**: anyone whose contribution has been merged.
- **Users**: anyone using the software under MIT/Apache-2.0.

## Changing this document

This document may be modified only by the founder. Changes are announced
in a public commit and in the project changelog.

It is a personal project that may collaborate with academic groups, but its
